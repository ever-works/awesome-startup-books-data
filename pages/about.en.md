# About Awesome Startup Books

Awesome Startup Books is a curated, continuously updated directory of books for founders, operators and anyone building a company — from the first idea to scaling a team.

## What you'll find here

- **Strategy and execution** — business strategy, disruption and innovation, growth and scaling.
- **Customers and go-to-market** — customer development, marketing and sales, customer experience.
- **Money and people** — fundraising and investment, leadership and management, productivity.
- **Stories** — biographies and first-hand accounts from founders and investors.

## How the directory is maintained

The directory is built and maintained with the [Ever Works](https://ever.works) platform. AI agents research new titles, keep existing listings current and organize everything into categories and tags; every listing links back to its source.
